AwowLab Local Windows log-review tool: builds a 3D replay from log data, an overlay damage meter reading your combat log live, and a pull-comparison view against a Warcraft Logs report. Only touches the network for updates, WCL pulls, or Wowhead lookups. Both
AwowlabOverlay (source) Open-source parser/overlay component behind AwowLab's standalone overlay. Both
Warcraft Recorder Auto-records boss fights, M+ dungeons, and arenas by watching the combat log, using OBS under the hood for capture. Requires the SimpleCombatLogger addon. Windows only, free and open source. Both
Archon App Desktop overlay showing player history and performance data right in Group Finder, plus log upload and analysis in desktop, always-on-top, or in-game display modes. Both
Raider.IO Desktop App Keeps the Raider.IO addon updated automatically and adds live tracking of M+ scores and raid progression outside the game. Both
WowUp Free, open-source addon manager — installs and updates addons from CurseForge, Wago, GitHub, and Tukui in one place. Both
CurseForge App Official Overwolf-built addon manager — browse, install, and update addons in one click. Both
